Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Wiccasoft, we are strictly committed to defending absolute digital sovereignty and user data protection. This Privacy Policy outlines our uncompromising No Track infrastructure built upon decentralized Web5 architecture.

1. Strict No-Track Architecture

Wiccasoft operates on a foundation of total privacy. We do not track, intercept, log, or store your browsing history, IP addresses, search queries, or peripheral digital footprints. Your interaction with the web remains completely unmonitored by our ecosystem.

2. Local Device Consciousness & Web5

Under our Web5 framework, all user data, cryptographic keys, and decentralized identities (DIDs) are kept exclusively within your local device consciousness. Because we do not utilize central corporate servers to harvest metrics, we hold zero capability to access, manipulate, or monetize your digital personal packets.

3. Information Collected by Third Parties (Ad Networks)

This website may integrate advertising solutions (such as Google AdSense) to keep our development lifecycle sustainable. These external networks may deploy standard tracking mechanics (such as cookies or web beacons) to deliver targeted ads based on your visits to this and other sites across the internet. Please note that Wiccasoft possesses no authority over these third-party behaviors.

4. Cookie Deployment

Our core platform does not implement proprietary data-mining cookies. However, third-party integrations (like Google AdSense or analytical scripts) might enforce their own cookie policies to enhance contextual relevance. 5. Data Security

Since your information never migrates to a Wiccasoft server, your security perimeter depends on your own local device environment. We urge our community to maintain robust personal device security protocols to protect their localized data structures.
